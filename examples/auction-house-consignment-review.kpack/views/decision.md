# Consignment Review — Committee Decision

## Recommendation

**Decline the consignment as submitted.** The committee's recommendation rests on the house's adopted cast-period assessment and the proposed terms. It can be reconsidered on revised terms (see Conditions).

## Basis of the assessment

- The work is a 38-cm bronze figure signed "Y." on the base, with a foundry mark identified as Foundry F, Paris.
- Foundry F began operating in 1925, twelve years after Sculptor Y's death. The house adopts a posthumous-cast assessment; the narrower 1925–approximately 1940 dating is the specialist's judgment from the mark variant.
- Vendor V's lifetime-cast attribution remains recorded as the consignor's position. The genuine 1971 catalog says "by Sculptor Y" but does not distinguish the cast period. The house reaches a different conclusion from its examination and foundry-mark evidence.
- The €180,000 reserve lies between the recorded lifetime-cast range (€320,000–€540,000) and posthumous-cast range (€80,000–€140,000). The house interprets the reserve as reflecting the consignor's attribution; other readings of intent remain possible.

## Why decline rather than re-list

Two paths were considered and rejected:

1. **List "by Sculptor Y" as submitted.** The house assesses reputational and potential legal risk from the attribution discrepancy. The nature and extent of legal exposure depend on jurisdiction and contract terms not fully analyzed in this pack.
2. **Change attribution without consignor sign-off.** The recorded agreement requires sign-off on attribution-language changes. The committee recommends obtaining agreement rather than changing the wording unilaterally; the pack does not independently determine legal breach or remedies.

The recommended path is to decline pending agreement to revised attribution and reserve terms. Revised attribution addresses the discrepancy; it does not establish that every risk is resolved.

## Conditions for reconsideration

The consignment may be reconsidered if the consignor agrees to:

- "After Sculptor Y, posthumous cast, Foundry F, c. 1925–1940" as the attribution.
- A reserve aligned with the recorded posthumous-cast market range (€80,000–€140,000).

## What remains in the record

The catalog and Vendor V's supplied family-provenance account remain available. They record the earlier attribution and the consignor's account; they do not establish lifetime authorship. The decline recommendation concerns this attribution and these terms, rather than a separate finding about the work's condition or quality.
