# Letter to the Consignor — Decline Pending Revised Terms

> Illustrative formal-register letter expressing the committee's recommendation.

---

Dear Vendor V,

Thank you for the opportunity to review the bronze sculpture for our forthcoming sale of nineteenth-century European works. We have examined the work, reviewed the foundry mark and consulted the published reference used for its identification.

Our specialist's assessment is that the work is a posthumous cast after Sculptor Y by Foundry F, Paris. The foundry began operating in 1925, after Sculptor Y's death in 1913. The narrower dating to approximately 1925–1940 is our specialist's judgment from the mark variant.

We retain your lifetime-cast attribution and the account you supplied of the work's family provenance. We also recognize that the genuine 1971 catalog describes it as "by Sculptor Y." That wording does not distinguish the cast period. Our different assessment does not erase that historical attribution or your account of it.

The recorded comparable Foundry F posthumous casts achieved hammer prices between €80,000 and €140,000 in 2024–2025. Our committee recommends declining the consignment as submitted, with reconsideration if you agree to the following terms:

1. Attribution as "after Sculptor Y, posthumous cast, Foundry F, c. 1925–1940."
2. A reserve aligned with the recorded posthumous-cast market range.

The recorded agreement requires your sign-off on attribution-language changes. Please let us know how you would like to proceed.

Yours sincerely,

The Committee
